# Code of Conduct

## Our promise

SVG Safe is a community project. We want everyone who reports a bug,
proposes a feature, reviews code or asks a question to feel welcome, whatever
their experience, background, identity or level of English.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and first pull requests.
- Assume good intent. Ask before you judge.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about code and ideas, not people.
- Accept that maintainers sometimes say no, and explain why when you do.
- Respect people's time: search existing issues and fill in the templates.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling and deliberate derailing.
- Sexualised language or attention.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Pressuring people to work for free or on your schedule.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests,
discussions, reviews and any place where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer,
Saizul Amin, through <https://saizul.com>, or use GitHub's "Report content"
feature. Reports are handled confidentially. Please include links or
screenshots if you can.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break this
code, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban anyone who does so,
depending on how serious and how repeated the behaviour is. Maintainers who
do not follow the code may be removed from the project.
